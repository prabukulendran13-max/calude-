How to play
  • Slide your striker along your baseline with the bar under the board, or drag it on the board. If a puck is in the way, it hops to the nearest open spot.
  • Pull back from the striker (or anywhere on the board) and let go to shoot. The arrow grows and turns red the harder you pull.
  • Pot one of yours and you go again. Keep potting to stack up a combo.
  • Pocket the striker and it's a foul: one of your pucks goes back to the middle.
  • You get 15 seconds a shot (the ring around your face). You can switch the timer off in the pause menu.
