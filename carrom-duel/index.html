How to play
  • Slide your striker along your baseline with the bar under the board, or drag it on the board.
  • Pull back from the striker (or from anywhere on the board) and let go to shoot. The arrow grows and turns red as you pull harder; the dotted line shows the direction.
  • You have 15 seconds per shot (the ring around your picture). You can turn the timer off in the game menu.
  • Pot one of your own pucks and you shoot again.
  • Pocketing the striker is a foul: you lose the turn and one of your potted pucks goes back to the centre.
  • You can't pot your opponent's last puck for them. It goes back to the centre.
